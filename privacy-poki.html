Short version

The game has no sign-in of its own and no purchases. We do not sell or share your personal data. Everything the game remembers about you it stores where you play it.

What is stored

Your display name — a callsign the game generates for you, which you can roll again on the title screen — and your avatar, your settings (language, sound and music volume, animations, orientation) and the current match autosave with your statistics. It is kept in your browser's local storage. Clearing the browser's site data deletes all of it; we hold no copy and cannot retrieve it for you.

Online play

Starting or joining an online match connects the game to Ably, a real-time messaging service. While the match runs, the name and avatar you chose and your moves travel between the players' devices through it, over an encrypted connection, and are not stored by us. Ably processes connection data such as your IP address under its own privacy policy. If you never start an online match, none of this leaves your device.

Poki

Ads are served only through the Poki SDK. Poki and its advertising partners may process technical data such as device type, browser and approximate location under their own Privacy Statement. This version contains no analytics of any kind — no Google Analytics, no GameAnalytics, no in-house tracking.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, write to us and we will remove it.
